    A joint session of the SouJava International Podcast and Quarkus Club, live on YouTube on Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 2PM Brasília time (UTC-3).

    AI code review prices keep climbing, while you get less say over which model reads your code and where it runs. Thiago Gonzaga, Staff Engineer and Oracle ACE Pro, decided to build their own reviewer.

    ThrillhouseBot is a self-hosted GitHub App that reviews pull requests. It is built with Java 25, Quarkus and LangChain4j, and ships as a GraalVM native binary. Bring your own key and pick the model that fits your budget, including local models with Ollama.

    There have been several releases since the July presentation, up to v0.9.3, and this session brings the new features, including:

    - `/improve` and `/generate-tests` commands
    - Review learnings from maintainer decisions
    - Scanning for leaked secrets and risky infrastructure settings
    - Linked issues and CI failures as review context
    - Model routing and fallback
    - A live review view

    The session also covers why Quarkus and LangChain4j, the architecture and its trade-offs, where AI helped build the bot and where it got in the way, and how to keep quality high with tests, dogfooding and a healthy distrust of generated code.
